Sunrise and sunset come from the NOAA solar position formula for the course coordinates, in local time with daylight saving.

The first tee is the latest of the fixed earliest time, sunrise plus the offset, and any late-start maintenance that weekday, rounded up to 5 minutes. The last 18 is the latest start that still finishes the full round by sunset plus the buffer.

With two-tee starts, every group off tee 1 reaches tee 10 one front-9 time later, and every 18-hole group off tee 10 comes back to tee 1 the same way. Those crossover slots are blocked. Two tees help most on short winter days; on long days they move capacity earlier without adding much.

Capacity figures are the theoretical maximum: every slot sold with a full group. The forecast applies the expected occupancy from the Fees tab.
