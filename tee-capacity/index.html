How the figures are worked out

Sunrise and sunset come from the NOAA solar position formula for the course coordinates, shown in local time with daylight saving.

The first tee time is the later of the fixed earliest time, sunrise plus the offset, and any late-start maintenance for that weekday, rounded up to 5 minutes. The last 18-hole tee time is the latest start that still finishes the full round by sunset (plus the buffer).

With two-tee starts, every group off tee 1 reaches tee 10 one 9-hole time later, and every 18-hole group off tee 10 comes back to tee 1 the same way. Those crossover slots are blocked. Two tees mainly help on short winter days; on long days they move capacity earlier without adding much.

Figures are a theoretical maximum: every slot sold with a full group. Multiply by your expected occupancy to forecast.
